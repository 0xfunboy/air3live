# Wardrobe Configuration: Crop Top and Boots

AIR3 Live defaults to the **Off-Shoulder Crop Top** and **Low-Cut Leather Boots** for 24/7 perpetual broadcasting.

## Garment Fitting Metrics

- **Crop Top**: Shorter vertical silhouette (13.45% effective reduction), elevated hem (+41.56 mm), straightened boat-neck neckline, and skinning weights normalized across 4 influence bones per vertex.
- **Leather Boots**: Two distinct fits are selectable:
  - `relaxed`: Natural relaxed boot shaft with subtle ankle slouch.
  - `low-cut`: Trimmed ankle cuff designed for streamlined clearance under trousers or skirts.

Both garments preserve native UV coordinates and high-resolution textures. Triangles beneath footwear and garments are masked via reversible draw index arrays, allowing instantaneous style switching without mesh reconstruction.
